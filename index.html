Our Story

Where It All Began

Shwe Pinya Nandaw Private High School was founded in 2018 by two visionary educators who believed that quality education should be accessible, holistic, and transformative. Starting with just 120 students and 8 teachers in a modest building in Balukhali, Camp 8E, we have grown into one of the region's most respected institutions.

Today, our campus serves over 1,000 students across Classes 1 through 12, with a faculty of 15+ qualified teachers, modern science and computer labs, a well-stocked library, and a vibrant cultural stage. Our alumni hold positions in medicine, engineering, civil service, arts, and entrepreneurship — a testament to the foundation we provide.

How to Apply

Admission Process

📋

Download Form

Collect the admission form from the school office or download it from our website. Fill it in carefully.

📁

Submit Documents

Submit the completed form along with previous year's report card, birth certificate, and 2 passport photos.

📝

Written Test

Appear for the admission test in Bangla, English, and Mathematics. Classes 6 & 9 are tested separately.

Admission Confirmed

Selected students receive a letter of admission. Complete fee payment within 7 days to secure your seat.

Common Questions

FAQ — Admissions

What is the minimum age for Class 6 admission? ▼
Students must be at least 11 years of age by the 1st of January of the admission year to enrol in Class 6.
Is there a waiting list policy? ▼
Yes, once seats are filled, qualified students are placed on a waiting list and contacted if vacancies arise. We encourage early application to avoid disappointment.
Can students transfer mid-year from another school? ▼
Mid-year transfers are considered subject to seat availability. The student must provide a transfer certificate and relevant academic records from their previous school.
Are there any discounts for siblings? ▼
Yes, a 10% discount on monthly tuition is offered for the second sibling enrolled simultaneously. Please mention this during the application process.
